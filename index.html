Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What’s the difference between free and paid courses?

A number of complete subjects are free to watch in full, so you can see how we teach before spending anything — but not the whole syllabus is free. Paid batches cover every subject and add the parts a video by itself can’t give you.

Free forever

What the free subjects include

  • Complete video lectures — the full subject, not a sample
  • Downloadable lecture notes for every topic
  • Lifetime access — rewatch anytime before your exam
What you pay for

Everything a video can’t do

  • A WhatsApp doubt group answered by Himanshu Sir and Anish Sir themselves
  • 10+ topic and subject tests per subject, written to GATE standard
  • Guidance on how to prepare, not just what to watch
What’s the difference between TITANS and Pathfinders batches?

Both cover the complete GATE syllabus with the same lecture quality. TITANS is the full-support batch, with placement prep, guidance and the test series built in.

See the full feature comparison in Full GATE Batches above, or read the ECE brochure and the EE brochure.

Confused about which course to buy?

Start with a free subject. Watch it end to end, see whether our teaching suits you, and only then decide — no card needed, no trial timer.

What payment methods do you support?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, through a secure checkout. It’s a one-time payment for the batch — nothing auto-renews.

Who answers my doubts?

Most doubts are answered by Himanshu Sir and Anish Sir themselves, in subject-specific WhatsApp and Telegram groups. We also have a dedicated doubt-solving team so nothing waits when the mentors are teaching. TITANS students additionally get bi-weekly 1:1 mentorship sessions.

Which platforms can I watch on?

Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, and directly on the website — use Google Chrome for the best experience. Offline downloads are available on the Android app.

I’m starting from zero. Will I be able to follow?

Yes. Every subject is built from fundamentals rather than as revision. Network Theory, for example, spends 13 hours on basics before touching transient analysis. If you have never seen the topic before, start at lecture one.

Which branches do you cover?

ECE and EE are fully covered, end to end. IN and BM are covered through the subjects they share with ECE and EE. General Aptitude is covered for every branch, and Engineering Mathematics is covered for:

EC, EE, IN, CS, ME, CE, CH and PI.

Are the lectures in Hindi or English?

Our courses are primarily taught in English. We have also introduced Hindi versions of selected courses, which are separately recorded in Hindi. However, the complete syllabus is available in English, and all courses are fully covered in English.

How long do I keep access?

Pathfinders runs until your GATE exam. TITANS runs 3 months longer, so it covers the post-result window for M.Tech/MS interviews and admissions. Free courses have lifetime access to the videos and notes.

Is the test series close to the real paper?

That is the part students mention most. Our AIR 9 from the TITANS batch described them as “very close to the actual GATE level,” and every question ships with a detailed solution so a wrong answer tells you what to fix.

Can I get a refund?

Please assume no, and buy accordingly. These are digital courses, so under our Refunds & Cancellation Policy a refund is only issued in two situations: the course you paid for was not assigned to your account within the expiry period, or you were charged twice for the same course. Change of mind, or having watched part of a course, is not refundable.

That is exactly why we put a free version of every core subject up, with the same lectures as the paid one. Watch that first and decide before you spend anything — it is a far more honest trial than a refund window would be.

Is there an app?

Yes — the same courses on every platform. Sign in with the same account you bought on:

  • Android — Google Play
  • iPhone & iPad — App Store
  • Mac — Apple silicon or Intel
  • Windows — download the installer
Can I download lectures and watch them offline?

Yes, on the Android app only — downloads are not available on iOS, Mac or Windows, where lectures stream normally.

One thing worth knowing: a downloaded lecture still needs a working internet connection to start playing, because the app checks your access before it opens the file. It only needs to be a connection, not a fast one — the video itself plays from the download and uses essentially no data. So a weak signal is fine; no signal at all is not.
