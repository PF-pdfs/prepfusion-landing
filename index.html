    Frequently asked questions

    Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp (messages only) or write to prepfusion.edu.contact@gmail.com.

    What’s the difference between free and paid courses?

    Several complete subjects are free to watch in full, so you can see how we teach before spending anything. Not the whole syllabus is free. Paid batches cover every subject and add what a video alone can’t give you.

    Free subjects include

    • Complete video lectures, the full subject, not a sample
    • Downloadable lecture notes for every topic
    • Lifetime access to rewatch before your exam

    Paid batches add

    • A WhatsApp doubt group answered by Himanshu Sir and Anish Sir themselves
    • 10+ topic and subject tests per subject, written to GATE standard
    • Guidance on how to prepare, not just what to watch
    What’s the difference between TITANS and Pathfinders?

    Both cover the complete GATE syllabus with the same lectures. TITANS is the full-support batch: 1:1 mentorship, the test series, the VLSI placement course and post-GATE guidance, and it runs 3 months longer. The comparison table lists every difference.

    Confused about which course to buy?

    Start with a free subject. Watch it end to end, see whether our teaching suits you, and only then decide. No card needed, no trial timer.

    Which branches do you cover?

    EC and EE are fully covered, end to end. IN and BM are covered through the subjects they share with EC and EE. General Aptitude is covered for every branch, and Engineering Mathematics for EC, EE, IN, CS, ME, CE, CH and PI.

    What payment methods do you support?

    UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, through a secure checkout. It’s a one-time payment for the batch; nothing auto-renews.

    Who answers my doubts?

    Most doubts are answered by Himanshu Sir and Anish Sir themselves, in subject-specific WhatsApp and Telegram groups. A dedicated doubt-solving team covers the hours when the mentors are teaching. TITANS students also get bi-weekly 1:1 mentorship sessions.

    Which platforms can I watch on?

    Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, Mac, and the website (Google Chrome works best). The download links are in Watch on any device.

    Can I download lectures and watch them offline?

    Yes, on the Android app only. Downloads are not available on iOS, Mac or Windows, where lectures stream.

    A downloaded lecture still needs a working internet connection to start playing, because the app checks your access before it opens the file. It only needs to be a connection, not a fast one: the video plays from the download and uses almost no data. A weak signal is fine; no signal at all is not.

    I’m starting from zero. Will I be able to follow?

    Yes. Every subject is built from fundamentals rather than as revision. Network Theory, for example, spends 13 hours on basics before touching transient analysis. If you have never seen the topic before, start at lecture one.

    Are the lectures in Hindi or English?

    Our courses are taught in English, and the complete syllabus is available in English. Selected courses also have separately recorded Hindi versions.

    How long do I keep access?

    Paid batches list an explicit validity date rather than a rolling subscription. TITANS runs longer than Pathfinders so that it covers the post-result window. Free courses have lifetime access to the videos and notes.

    Is the test series close to the real paper?

    That is the part students mention most. Our AIR 9 from the TITANS batch called it “very close to the actual GATE level”, and every question comes with a detailed solution, so a wrong answer tells you what to fix.

    Can I get a refund?

    Please assume no, and buy accordingly. These are digital courses, so under our Refunds and Cancellation Policy a refund is issued in only two situations: the course you paid for was not assigned to your account within the expiry period, or you were charged twice for the same course. A change of mind, or having watched part of a course, is not refundable.

    That is why several complete subjects are free, with the same lectures as the paid batches. Watch one first and decide before you spend anything.
