Questions

Everything else worth asking.

If some subjects are free, why would I pay for the rest?

Because lectures are only half of preparing. Paid batches cover the full syllabus and add a WhatsApp doubt group answered by Himanshu Sir and Anish Sir directly, assignments with solved PYQs worked through in class, and on TITANS specifically — live guidance sessions, 1:1 mentorship and a full test series.

If you just want to see how we teach first, start with a free subject — no card, no catch.

I’m starting from zero. Will I be able to follow?

Yes. Every subject is built from fundamentals rather than as revision. Network Theory, for example, spends 13 hours on basics before touching transient analysis. If you have never seen the topic before, start at lecture one.

Which branches do you cover?

ECE, EE and IN are fully covered, and BM shares most of the core subjects. General Aptitude and Engineering Mathematics apply to every branch.

Are the lectures in Hindi or English?

Both, depending on the course. Each course page lists its mode of communication, and some core subjects — Network Theory among them — have a complete Hindi version taught separately, not dubbed.

How does doubt-solving actually work?

You're added to a subject-specific WhatsApp or Telegram group — Analog, Digital, EMFT, Maths & Aptitude, Signals & Systems and more each have their own — monitored by the mentors, not a support desk. TITANS students also get bi-weekly 1:1 mentorship sessions on top of the group.

Can I watch on my phone, and can I download lectures?

Yes — the site works on any browser, and we have apps for Windows, Mac, iOS and Android. Downloading for offline viewing is available on the Android app only; a few older videos and any YouTube-linked content aren't downloadable.

How long do I keep access?

Paid batches list an explicit validity date rather than a rolling subscription — TITANS runs longer than Pathfinders specifically so it covers the post-result window. Free courses have lifetime access to the videos and notes.

Is the test series close to the real paper?

That is the part students mention most. Our AIR 9 from the TITANS batch described them as “very close to the actual GATE level,” and every question ships with a detailed solution so a wrong answer tells you what to fix.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are governed by our published Refunds & Cancellation Policy, linked in the footer. Since every core subject has a free version with identical lectures, we strongly suggest watching that first — it is the most honest trial we can offer.
